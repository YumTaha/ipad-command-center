Display settings

Calendar settings and imported events stay in this browser. Use a separate display calendar or a private proxy with only the events you need.

Endpoint must allow CORS for this site's origin and the Authorization header. Credentials stored locally can be read by anyone with access to this device.

Public display calendars work best. Signed-in private embeds may fail in old Safari due to iframe cookies or Google sign-in support.

Private local snapshot: imported events display as Busy. Includes the next 30 days; import again for changes or later dates. Supports Outlook embedded time zones and daily, weekly, monthly or yearly recurrence. Unsupported cases fail visibly; nothing is uploaded.

Automatic night dimming: 10 PM–7 AM Eastern. The dim button cycles Auto, On and Off. Brightness, Auto-Lock and Guided Access are set on the iPad.
